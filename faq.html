Frequently Asked Questions

Honest answers to the questions men actually ask.

If you don’t see your question here, reach out—we’d rather answer directly than leave you guessing.

Leaving Egypt is a Christ-centered men’s organization and brotherhood. We help men leave behind what has kept them stuck and grow in four areas that shape a man’s life: Faith, Family, Fitness, and Finance. We do that through biblical teaching, honest friendship, and practical steps.

It’s for adult men at any stage of faith who want to grow—whether you feel spiritually distant, carry regret, feel isolated, or simply sense that something needs to change. You don’t need to have it all together. You just need to be willing to take an honest first step.
