            <p class="eyebrow">Why brotherhood matters</p>
            <h2 id="why-h">Men change faster when someone walks with them</h2>
            <p class="mt-1 muted">
              Isolation is where good intentions go to die. A man can decide to
              change on a Sunday and forget it by Wednesday if no one else
              knows. Brotherhood puts honest, encouraging people around your
              goals—so the truth gets spoken, the wins get celebrated, and the
              follow-through actually happens.
            </p>
            <p class="muted">
              Scripture puts it simply: iron sharpens iron (Proverbs 27:17).
              We're built to grow in the company of others.
            </p>
