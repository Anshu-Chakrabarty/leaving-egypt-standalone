Our Commitment

We’re committed to making this website usable for as many people as possible, including people who rely on assistive technology. We view accessibility as an ongoing effort, not a one-time task.

Standard We Aim For

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA where reasonably possible. This includes clear heading structure, keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus states, sufficient color contrast, descriptive links, proper form labels, alt text for meaningful images, and support for reduced-motion preferences.

No Guarantee of Perfection

While we work toward these goals, we can’t guarantee that every page is fully accessible at all times, or that it works perfectly with every device or assistive technology. We’re always working to improve.
